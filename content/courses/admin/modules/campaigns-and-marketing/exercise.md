## Scenario

Khanyisa Solar runs five roadshows across Gauteng. Marketing wants to know sign-ups, attendance and revenue per roadshow and for the whole series.

## Your goal

Build the campaign structure and report on it.

## Before you start

- A Developer Edition org or Trailhead Playground.
- A CSV of 30 fake attendees, split into leads and contacts.

## What to build

1. A parent campaign **Spring Roadshows** and five child campaigns.
2. For the children, add statuses **Registered, Attended, Quote Requested** and mark Attended and Quote Requested as Responded.
3. Fill in budgeted and actual cost and expected revenue.
4. **Import** campaign members with the Data Import Wizard, and add some from a list view.
5. Convert two leads into opportunities linked to the campaign and mark one Closed Won.
6. Turn on **Campaign Influence** and run the standard campaign reports.
7. Build a **dashboard** of pipeline by campaign.

## What you should see when you are done

- [ ] The parent shows rolled-up counts.
- [ ] Response counts only include statuses flagged Responded.
- [ ] The won opportunity is credited to the campaign.
- [ ] The dashboard shows pipeline per campaign.

## Check your understanding

- [ ] Which record types can be campaign members?
- [ ] What does the Responded flag on a member status do?
- [ ] How does campaign hierarchy help marketing?

## Stretch goal

Create a report type that shows campaigns with their members and their conversion status.
