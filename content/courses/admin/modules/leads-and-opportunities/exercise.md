## Scenario

Khanyisa Solar sells residential and commercial installations. Residential skips a formal proposal stage. Web leads must go to a team queue, and reps want a quote PDF to send.

## Your goal

Set up the sales process from lead to quote.

## Before you start

- A Developer Edition org or Trailhead Playground.
- Two test users in a public group.

## What to build

1. A **Web-to-Lead** form and a **queue** "New Web Leads". Create an **assignment rule** that sends web leads to the queue.
2. Add a custom lead field **System Size** and **map it** to a custom Opportunity field so it carries over on conversion.
3. **Convert** a lead into an account, contact and opportunity. Open the converted lead and try to edit it.
4. Two **sales processes** ("Residential" and "Commercial") and two **record types** on Opportunity with different stage lists. Add stage probabilities and forecast categories.
5. Products "5kW Kit" and "10kW Kit" in the **standard price book**, then a custom price book "Commercial 2026".
6. Add products to an opportunity, create a **quote** and generate a **PDF**.
7. A **Path** on Opportunity with guidance for each stage.

## What you should see when you are done

- [ ] A web lead lands in the queue.
- [ ] System Size appears on the opportunity after conversion.
- [ ] Residential and Commercial opportunities show different stage lists.
- [ ] You can generate a quote PDF.

## Check your understanding

- [ ] What can you no longer do with a converted lead?
- [ ] Where does the list of stages live: record type or sales process?
- [ ] What must exist before a product can go into a custom price book?

## Stretch goal

Enable collaborative forecasts and compare Commit and Best Case on your test opportunities.
